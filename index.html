FAQ

Who is the professional membership for?

GrowthMinded is built for school counselors, mental health and licensed counselors, social workers, school psychologists, and other professionals who support children's emotional growth and life skills in schools, practices, or group settings.

Can I use this across my whole caseload?

Yes. Your individual license covers you as one professional across as many students and clients as you support — classroom guidance lessons, small groups, and 1:1 sessions — under a single login, with no per-student cap. Usage rights stay active as long as your membership does.

Is the content aligned with MTSS and SEL standards?

Resources are organized to support Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 work, and the Growth Path gives you a developmental progression to plan and measure against.

What ages is the content for?

Early elementary through teens and young adults — with strong support for teens and young adults, the group most often underserved. Every resource is labeled by age and grade band so you can match the right tool to the right student.

Do I need to buy the journals or kits separately?

No. GrowthMinded is your complete library — the Big Life Journal kits are already included. There's nothing else to buy.

Is there a curriculum with ready-to-teach lesson plans?

Yes. GrowthMinded includes 15-minute lesson plans built on the Growth Path, each with Google Slides you can present as-is. The Emotional Intelligence unit is available now for every grade level, with more units rolling out.

Can I share resources with parents and families?

Yes. You can share printables and handouts directly with the students and families you work with.

Can my counseling team or district share one membership?

An individual membership is a single login for one professional. For departments, schools, or districts, our Teams & Schools plan adds multi-user access — book a call to find the right fit.

Can my school or district pay by purchase order?

Yes. We accept purchase orders and can provide a quote and a W-9 so your school or district can cover your membership. Just ask when you book a call.

Can I use it on my mobile device, or without a printer?

Yes. GrowthMinded works on mobile, and many videos and visuals can be used on-screen in session without printing.

What is the refund and cancellation policy?

GrowthMinded comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee — if it's not the right fit, request a full refund within 30 days (available if you've downloaded fewer than 10 resources). You can cancel your membership at any time to stop future renewals.
